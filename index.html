Zaldar87 Shorts Studio

Zaldar87 Shorts Studio is a free desktop app for Windows and Mac that turns your gameplay clips into vertical shorts and a weekly compilation video. It runs on your own computer. You review every video, and nothing is posted until you approve it.

What it does

  • Finds the best moments in the clips you drop into a folder
  • Cuts them into shorts with captions, music and an end card
  • Builds a weekly compilation from the shorts you approved
  • Optional: uploads the videos you approved to your own YouTube channel, on the schedule you set

Why it asks for access to your YouTube account

Uploading is optional. If you turn it on, you sign in with Google and the app asks for two permissions:

  • Upload videos. Used to upload the videos you approved to your channel, with their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • View your YouTube account. Used only to read your channel's name and ID, so the app can show you which channel is linked.

The app does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers or analytics, and it never deletes or changes videos already on your channel. Your sign-in is kept on your own computer. The maker of the app never receives it.

You can remove the app's access at any time: in the app under Setup, Auto upload, Unlink YouTube, or on Google's security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
