Privacy Policy for Zaldar87 Shorts Studio

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Zaldar87 Shorts Studio ("the app") is made by Zaldar Entertainment LLC ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

The app runs on your own computer. We do not run a server for it. We do not receive, collect, store or sell your information. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to your YouTube channel when you turn that feature on. YouTube is a Google service. The Google Privacy Policy is at http://www.google.com/policies/privacy. By using the app's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

What the app accesses in your Google account

Nothing, until you sign in with Google and allow it. After that:

  • Your YouTube channel's name and ID. Read when you link the account, and shown in the app so you can see which channel is linked.
  • Permission to upload videos to your channel. For each video you approved, the app sends the video file, title, description, tags, thumbnail, category, and the visibility and publish time you chose. If you pick or change a video's thumbnail after it was uploaded, the app sends the new thumbnail for that video.

The app does not access your email, contacts, other videos, comments, subscribers or analytics.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored on your own computer:

  • Your Google sign-in (a refresh token), kept in your computer's password store (Windows Credential Manager or macOS Keychain). On a computer with no password store it is kept in a file in the app's folder.
  • Your channel's name and ID.
  • A list of the videos the app uploaded, with their YouTube links, so that no video is uploaded twice.

None of this is sent to us.

How the information is used and shared

  • It is used only to upload the videos you approved and to show you which channel is linked.
  • It is sent only to YouTube (Google), over an encrypted connection, when you link your account and when a video is uploaded.
  • We do not sell it, share it or transfer it to anyone else. It is not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.

Other services you may connect

Each of these is used only if you set it up, and each has its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Anthropic (Claude). To edit your videos, the app sends frames, transcripts and pictures from your own clips to Anthropic's API using your own API key. Information from your Google account is not sent to Anthropic.
  • Twitch. If you link it, the app reads the list of your own channel's clips and downloads the ones it picks.
  • Bluesky. If you link it, the app posts the videos you approved.

Cookies and similar technologies

The app does not use cookies to track you. Its dashboard is a page served by your own computer and cannot be reached from the internet. It uses your browser's local storage only for small conveniences, such as remembering which microphone you picked.

Removing access and deleting your data

  • In the app: Setup, Auto upload, Unlink YouTube. This asks Google to cancel the sign-in and removes it from your computer.
  • At Google: remove the app on the security settings page at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • To delete everything the app stored, delete the app's folder and its studio folder. We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Videos that were already uploaded stay on YouTube until you delete them there.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
